What it actually is

Your life, placed beside the Tree of Life

Kabbalah has an old and beautiful diagram of the human soul — the Sefirot, ten living qualities arranged as the Tree of Life: understanding, loving-kindness, strength, beauty, endurance, foundation, and more. For centuries it has been a way of seeing where a life is bound, and where it is ready to open.

Your Map takes your own words — your story, in your answers — and reflects them through that ancient structure. Written by hand, for you alone, it shows you three things clearly:

  • Your patterns — the inherited rules and quiet habits that have been steering you, and where they truly began. Not flaws. Simply the shape you've been living inside.
  • Your strengths — the aliveness, courage, and gifts that never actually left you, even in the years you couldn't feel them.
  • Your next step — one specific, doable move toward the life you want, chosen for your life, not a general one.

It arrives as a beautifully typeset keepsake — yours to print, to keep, and to return to whenever you lose your footing.
